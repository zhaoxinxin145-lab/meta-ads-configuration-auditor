Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Requesting Deletion

To request deletion of data stored by Meta Ads Configuration Auditor, contact the application operator at YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL. Describe the information you want deleted. Do not include passwords, access tokens or other authentication secrets.

Local Audit Reports

Excel reports downloaded to your computer are controlled by the operator. Delete these files from your computer and any other locations where copies were saved.

Application-Managed Data

The operator will review deletion requests and delete eligible application-managed data within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

If the tool does not retain a server-side copy of a report, there may be no server-side report to delete.
